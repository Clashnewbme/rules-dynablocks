DynaBlocks Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 11, 2026
Last Updated: October 8, 2026, 1:16 PM EST.

This Privacy Policy explains how DynaBlocks collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you use the website at www.dynablocks.org, the DynaBlocks client software, and the games, experiences and related services we provide (together, the "Platform").

The Platform is used by teenagers as well as adults. Sections 4, 12, 13 and 14 are written particularly for teenagers, parents and guardians, and we encourage you to read them.

1. About This Policy

1.1 This Policy tells you what personal information we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and what rights you have.

1.2 It applies to personal information we collect through the Platform and through communications with us. It does not apply to third party websites or services that we do not control, including services you reach through a link from the Platform.

1.3 "Personal information" means information that identifies, relates to, describes, or could reasonably be linked with an identified or identifiable individual. It has the same meaning as "personal data" where that term is used in the law of your country.

2. Who We Are and How to Contact Us

2.1 DynaBlocks is the controller of the personal information described in this Policy, and is the "business" for the purposes of California law and the "operator" for the purposes of the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act.

2.2 For privacy, general support, copyright, intellectual property, or other privacy-related requests, please use the official DynaBlocks support channels provided on the Platform.

2.3 Parents and guardians should use the official DynaBlocks support channels for any request concerning a child's personal information, including a request to review, delete or stop the further collection of that information.

3. Scope

3.1 The Platform is operated from the United States and is available to users worldwide. This Policy covers every user of the Platform wherever they are located. Our user communities may include users in the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, the European Union and other countries.

3.2 Because we operate globally, your personal information may be processed in the United States regardless of where you live. Section 16 explains how international transfers are handled.

3.3 Where the law of your country gives you rights or protections beyond those described here, we will honor those rights and protections. Sections 21 to 23 describe rights that apply in particular countries and states.

4. A Note for Teenagers, Parents and Guardians

4.1 If you are a teenager, this section explains the important parts in plain terms. We collect the information we need to give you an account, let you play and create, keep you safe, and let you use Platform features and make purchases. Other people can see what you post and what you say in chat. Moderators and automated systems may check chat and content to keep the Platform safe, so what you type or say is not private. Never share your real name, address, school, phone number or passwords with anyone on the Platform. If something makes you uncomfortable, use the report feature or tell a trusted adult.

4.2 Minimum age. The Platform is not intended for, and we do not knowingly allow, anyone under the age of 13 to use it or create an Account. We may ask for your date of birth when you register to help us enforce this. Other than the limited information we are permitted to collect to determine a user's age, we do not knowingly collect personal information from a person under 13. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a person under 13, we will delete it promptly and close the Account, subject to applicable law.

4.3 Where the law of your country sets a higher digital age of consent for certain kinds of processing, processing that relies on your consent, such as non-essential cookies or marketing, may require the consent of a parent or legal guardian, even though you meet our general minimum age of 13.

4.4 We do not condition a teenager's participation in an activity on disclosing more personal information than is reasonably necessary for that activity.

4.5 If you are a parent or legal guardian and have a concern about a minor's Account, including a request to review, delete, or stop the further collection of a minor's personal information, or a belief that an Account belongs to a person under 13, contact DynaBlocks through the official support channels. We will investigate and take appropriate action, which may include deleting the personal information we hold and closing the Account.

5. Categories of Personal Information We Collect

5.1 We may collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Identifiers and account information: username, email address, password in hashed form, account identifier, date of birth or age, parent or guardian contact details where applicable, and account identifiers from third-party services where you choose to link them.
  • Profile and avatar information: avatar configuration, profile description, friends and followers, groups and communities you belong to.
  • User generated content: games and experiences, avatar accessories and other virtual items, images, audio, video, three dimensional models, scripts and code, and the metadata associated with them.
  • Communications content: text chat, private messages, group messages, and, where the feature is available to you, voice chat audio and any transcription of it.
  • Commercial information: purchases of DynaBucks, subscriptions, virtual item purchases, trades, gifts, creator earnings, refund and chargeback history, and, in future, payout records.
  • Payment information: billing details and a payment method token supplied by our payment processor. We do not store your full payment card number.
  • Device, network and technical information: device and hardware identifiers, operating system and version, client software version, browser type, screen and hardware configuration, crash and diagnostic data, internet protocol address and the approximate location derived from it, and timestamps.
  • Usage information: the experiences you play and for how long, features you use, in-Platform actions, session information and interaction data.
  • Safety and enforcement information: reports you make or that are made about you, moderation decisions, warning and ban history, ban evasion signals, and the device and network identifiers used to enforce restrictions.
  • Age assurance information: where age assurance is applied to you, the signals and any documentation or estimate used to determine your age, and the result.
  • Verification information for creator payouts: if and when the program becomes available to you, legal name, address, government identifier and tax documentation.
  • Correspondence: the content of your support, appeal, privacy and copyright communications with us.

5.2 We do not collect precise geolocation from your device. We may infer approximate location from your internet protocol address.

5.3 We do not use facial recognition, fingerprint scanning or similar biometric identification on the Platform. If we introduce identity or age verification that involves a biometric process operated by a third-party provider, we will update this Policy, tell you before it applies to you, obtain any consent required by law, and describe how long the information is kept and when it is destroyed.

10. Legal Bases for Processing

10.1 If you are in the United Kingdom, the European Union or the European Economic Area, we rely on the following legal bases under applicable data protection law:

  • Performance of a contract: to create and operate your account, provide the Platform, process purchases and subscriptions, and deliver the features you ask for.

  • Legitimate interests: to keep the Platform and its users safe, moderate content, prevent fraud and abuse, secure our systems, understand and improve how the Platform is used, and establish, exercise or defend legal claims.
  • Consent: for non-essential cookies and similar technologies, analytics and advertising technologies where consent is required, processing where parental consent is required, and anything else for which we ask your consent.

10.3 Where you are in the United States, state privacy laws generally do not require us to identify a specific legal basis for each use of personal information in the same way that European data protection laws do. 12.2 These tools can result in content being blocked, filtered, hidden or removed, features being restricted, and accounts being suspended or terminated, in some cases without a person reviewing the decision first.

19. Data Retention

19.1 We keep personal information only for as long as is reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, and then delete it or anonymize it where appropriate.

19.5 Backups are overwritten on a rolling cycle, and information deleted from live systems may persist in backups until the relevant backup is overwritten.
