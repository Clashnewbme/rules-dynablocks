Effective Date: March 11, 2026
Last Updated: October 8, 2026, 1:16 PM EST.

This Cookie Policy explains how DynaBlocks uses cookies and similar technologies on the website at www.dynablocks.org and in connection with the DynaBlocks platform. It should be read together with the DynaBlocks Privacy Policy.

1. What Cookies Are

1.1 A cookie is a small text file placed on your device by a website. Similar technologies include pixels, local storage, software development kits and device identifiers, which perform comparable functions. In this Policy, "cookies" covers all of them.

1.2 A first party cookie is set by the website you are visiting. A third party cookie is set by another organization whose service is used on that website. A session cookie is deleted when you close your browser. A persistent cookie remains until it expires or you delete it.

2. Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies to keep you signed in, to keep your account and our systems secure, to remember your settings, to make the website work correctly, and to understand how the website is used so that we can improve it.

3. Categories of Cookies We Use

3.1 Strictly necessary cookies. These are required for the website to function and cannot be switched off in our systems. They support authentication and session management, security and fraud prevention, load balancing, and remembering your cookie preferences. These cookies do not require your consent.

3.2 Functional cookies. These remember choices you make, such as language and display preferences, so the website behaves the way you expect.

3.3 Analytics cookies. We may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors find and use the website, which pages are viewed, and where problems occur. The information is used in aggregate to improve the website. Where consent is required in your country, these cookies are set only after you consent.

3.4 Advertising cookies. We do not currently use advertising cookies. If we introduce advertising, potentially including Google advertising services, we will update this Policy before doing so, will present advertising cookies for consent where consent is required, and will apply the restrictions on advertising to minors described in the Privacy Policy.

4. Third Parties

The third parties whose technologies may be present on the website are our hosting and security providers, our payment processor Stripe, Inc. in connection with payment pages, Google, in connection with Google Analytics, and hCaptcha, in connection with bot and spam prevention on registration, login and password reset. Those parties process information in accordance with their own privacy notices.

5. The DynaBlocks Client

The DynaBlocks client software is not a web browser and does not use browser cookies. It may store data locally on your device, including a session token, cached game assets, your settings, and diagnostic logs. This local data is necessary for the client to run.

6. Your Choices

6.1 Where a consent tool is presented to you, you can accept or reject non-essential categories, and you can change your choice at any time through the cookie settings link on the website.

6.2 You can control cookies through your browser settings, including deleting existing cookies and blocking new ones. Blocking strictly necessary cookies will prevent parts of the website from working, including signing in.

6.3 You can opt out of Google Analytics on any browser by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

6.4 Where the law requires it, we honor opt-out preference signals sent by your browser, including the Global Privacy Control.

7. Children

We do not use cookies to build advertising or marketing profiles of children. Cookies set in connection with a child's use of the website are limited to those necessary to operate the website, to keep the account secure, and to support internal operations including fraud prevention and enforcement.

8. Changes to This Policy

We will update this Policy when our use of cookies changes, and will change the Last Updated date. Where a change requires consent, we will ask for it before the change takes effect.

9. Contact

Questions about this Policy can be sent through the DynaBlocks support channels listed on the website.
