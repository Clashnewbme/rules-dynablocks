DynaBlocks Refund Policy

Effective Date: 11 August 2026
Last Updated: 11 August 2026

This Refund Policy explains when DynaBlocks provides refunds for purchases made at www.dynablocks.org and within the DynaBlocks platform. It forms part of the DynaBlocks Terms of Service. Nothing in this Policy takes away a right that the consumer law of your country gives you and that cannot lawfully be excluded.

1. What This Policy Covers

This Policy covers purchases of DynaBucks, virtual items, avatar items, experience access and other digital content purchased through DynaBlocks, as well as paid subscriptions or premium memberships that DynaBlocks may offer.

Avatar and virtual items include, but are not limited to, hats, accessories, shirts, clothing, avatar items, cosmetic items and other digital items purchased or obtained through the DynaBlocks Shop or other Platform features.

2. General Refund Position

2.1 DynaBucks, virtual items, avatar items, experience access and other digital content may be made available to you immediately after purchase. Once a purchase is completed and the digital content has been delivered to your account, the purchase is generally non-refundable, subject to the rest of this Policy and any statutory rights that apply to you.

2.2 A purchase gives you a license to use the applicable digital content within DynaBlocks. It does not give you ownership of DynaBlocks' intellectual property and does not give DynaBucks or virtual items a cash value.

2.3 Purchases of hats, accessories, shirts, clothing, avatar items, cosmetic items and similar virtual items are final. We do not provide refunds simply because you changed your mind, no longer want the item, purchased the wrong item, purchased an item accidentally, or no longer use the item.

2.4 We do not refund DynaBucks or virtual items that are lost through a trade you regret, a scam by another user, off-Platform trading, sharing your account credentials, or an enforcement action taken against your account for breaking our rules, except where a refund is required by applicable law.

3. In-App and Shop Purchases

3.1 Purchases made through the DynaBlocks Shop, avatar editor, in-experience shop or other Platform purchasing systems are generally final once the purchased content has been delivered to your account.

3.2 This includes purchases of hats, accessories, shirts, clothing, avatar cosmetics, virtual items, DynaBucks and other digital goods.

3.3 We do not refund an item because it does not match the way you expected it to look on your avatar, because you changed your avatar afterward, because you purchased it by mistake, or because you simply do not like the item.

3.4 Before completing a purchase, you are responsible for checking the item, its description, its price and any other information presented to you.

4. When We Will Give a Refund

Subject to applicable law, we may provide a refund where:

  • you were charged twice for the same purchase;
  • you were charged an amount other than the price shown at the time of purchase;
  • a technical fault caused a purchase to fail and the purchased content was not delivered;
  • the digital content you purchased was materially different from what was described and we cannot reasonably correct the problem;
  • the purchase was made without authorization, including an unauthorized purchase made by a minor;
  • the purchase was processed incorrectly by DynaBlocks; or
  • a refund or other remedy is required by the consumer law that applies to you.

A refund under this section does not mean that ordinary purchases of hats, accessories, shirts, clothing or other avatar items become refundable simply because the purchaser changes their mind.

5. Statutory Cancellation and Refund Rights

5.1 United Kingdom and European Union. If you are a consumer in the United Kingdom or European Union, mandatory consumer protection and digital-content laws may provide cancellation, refund or other rights. Where applicable law permits us to obtain your express consent to immediate delivery of digital content and requires an acknowledgement that a cancellation right will be lost once supply begins, we may request that consent at the point of purchase. Nothing in this Policy removes a right that cannot legally be excluded.

5.2 Subscriptions. Where a subscription is offered, applicable cancellation rights depend on the law governing your purchase and the circumstances in which the subscription was started. Where required by law, we will provide the applicable cancellation or refund rights.

5.3 Australia. Our services may be subject to guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. Nothing in this Policy limits rights or remedies that cannot legally be excluded.

5.4 New Zealand. If you are a consumer in New Zealand, applicable consumer protection laws may provide rights in addition to those described in this Policy. Nothing in this Policy limits rights that cannot legally be excluded.

5.5 Canada and the United States. Consumer protection law in your province, territory or state may provide rights beyond those described in this Policy. Nothing here limits rights that cannot legally be excluded.

5.6 Other countries. DynaBlocks is available worldwide. If you are a consumer resident in another country, you have the benefit of any mandatory consumer protection rules applicable to you, including any statutory cancellation, refund, repair or replacement right that cannot lawfully be excluded.

6. Purchases Made by Minors

6.1 If a minor makes a purchase using your payment method without your permission, contact DynaBlocks support as soon as you become aware of the purchase and provide the account username, date and amount of the charge, and the circumstances.

6.2 We will review unauthorized purchase claims in good faith. Where the circumstances support the claim, we may refund the purchase and remove the corresponding DynaBucks or virtual items from the account.

6.3 We may decline a request where the purchase appears to have been authorized, where the content has already been consumed or transferred, where the account has a history of unauthorized purchase claims, or where applicable law does not require a refund.

6.4 Parents and guardians remain responsible for any payment method they make available to a minor and should take reasonable steps to prevent unauthorized purchases.

7. Subscriptions

7.1 Where DynaBlocks offers subscriptions, they renew automatically until canceled unless the applicable purchase terms state otherwise.

7.2 You can cancel a subscription through the account settings or cancellation mechanism provided by DynaBlocks.

7.3 Cancellation normally takes effect at the end of the current paid period. You keep access to the subscription until then. We do not provide partial refunds for unused time except where required by law, where a technical fault prevented access, or where another applicable refund right applies.

7.4 If you purchase a subscription through a third-party application store or payment provider, that provider's refund and cancellation rules may also apply.

8. How to Request a Refund

8.1 Contact DynaBlocks through the support channels provided on the website. Include your username, the email address associated with the account, the date and amount of the purchase, the item or subscription concerned, and an explanation of the problem.

Do not send your full payment card number, password or other sensitive account credentials.

8.2 We aim to acknowledge refund requests within 3 business days and to make a decision within 14 days where reasonably possible. Where a statutory time limit applies, we will comply with the applicable requirement.

8.3 Approved refunds are normally made to the original payment method. The time required for a refund to appear depends on your bank, card issuer or payment provider.

8.4 Where we refund a purchase, we may remove the corresponding DynaBucks, virtual items or other digital content from the account.

9. Chargebacks

9.1 Please contact DynaBlocks before disputing a charge with your bank or card issuer where possible. Many payment problems can be resolved more quickly through support.

9.2 Where a chargeback is raised, we may restrict the account and associated DynaBucks or virtual items while the dispute is being investigated.

9.3 Where a chargeback is determined to have been made fraudulently or in circumstances where the purchase was authorized and delivered, we may take enforcement action against the account in accordance with the Terms of Service.

10. Fraud and Abuse

We may decline a refund request that we reasonably believe is fraudulent, forms part of a pattern of purchasing and reclaiming, or relates to content obtained in breach of the Terms of Service or Community Guidelines. Nothing in this Section limits a statutory right.

11. Changes and Contact

11.1 We may update this Policy when our refund practices or applicable Platform features change. The current version will be made available on the DynaBlocks website.

11.2 Questions about refunds should be submitted through the DynaBlocks support channels provided on the website.
