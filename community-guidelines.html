DynaBlocks Community Guidelines

Effective Date: 11 August 2026
Last Updated: 11 August 2026

DynaBlocks is a place where teenagers and adults build games, make things for their avatars, play together and talk to each other. Many of our users are teenagers. That fact shapes every rule in this document.

These Community Guidelines form part of the DynaBlocks Terms of Service. Breaking them can cost you your content, your features, your virtual items and your account.

1. Purpose and Scope

1.1 These Guidelines set the standard for content and behavior on DynaBlocks. They exist to keep the Platform safe, particularly for the youngest people using it, and to make it a place worth creating in.

1.2 They apply everywhere on the Platform: experiences and games, avatar accessories and other user generated items, images, audio, video, three dimensional models, scripts and code, text chat, voice chat, private messages, group and community content, usernames, profile text, thumbnails, descriptions and anything else you make available.

1.3 They also apply to off-Platform conduct where that conduct creates a risk to DynaBlocks users, particularly conduct involving children, threats of violence, or coordinated attempts to break these Guidelines on the Platform.

1.4 DynaBlocks is available worldwide. These Guidelines apply to you wherever you are, and they apply in addition to, not instead of, the law of your own country. Where local law imposes a stricter requirement on content or conduct than these Guidelines do, that stricter requirement applies to you.

1.5 These Guidelines are not exhaustive. We will act on conduct that is clearly harmful even where no specific rule names it, and we will interpret these Guidelines in the way that best protects users, especially children.

2. Who These Guidelines Apply To

2.1 Everyone with a DynaBlocks account, including players, creators, developers, group owners and moderators, and including a parent or guardian involved in a minor's use of the Platform.

2.2 If you publish an experience, you are responsible for what happens in it. That includes the content you put there, the scripts you run, the items you sell and the communication features you switch on. Building a space that invites rule-breaking is itself a breach of these Guidelines.

2.3 If you run a group or community, you are responsible for its name, description, imagery and public content, and you are expected to act on serious breaches by its members when you become aware of them.

3. Core Principles

3.1 Assume a child is in the room. Whatever you post, say, build or sell, assume a young child will encounter it.

3.2 Be someone others can play with. Disagreement is fine. Cruelty is not.

3.3 Make things honestly. Create your own work, credit what you are permitted to use, and describe what you sell accurately.

3.4 Play the game as built. Do not modify the client, exploit faults, or automate what is meant to be done by a person.

3.5 Report rather than retaliate. If something is wrong, use the report tools. Do not take enforcement into your own hands.

4. The Age-Appropriate Content Standard

4.1 DynaBlocks applies a single baseline standard across the Platform: content must be suitable for a general audience that includes children. Where the Platform offers a maturity label, age gate or content rating, you must apply the one that honestly reflects your content, and applying a label does not permit content that these Guidelines prohibit outright.

4.2 Content that is lawful for adults elsewhere on the internet may still be prohibited here. The test is not "is this legal", it is "is this appropriate for the audience that can reach it on DynaBlocks".

4.3 Mild cartoon or fantasy conflict of the kind found in games made for children is acceptable. Realistic injury, blood and gore, and violence presented for its own sake are not.

4.4 Do not use suggestive framing, coded language, deliberate misspellings, symbols, alternative alphabets, images of text, audio, or asset names to sneak prohibited content past our filters. Evasion is treated as an aggravating factor, not a defense.

5. Prohibited Content

5.1 You must not create, upload, publish, share, sell, link to or promote content that:

  • is illegal under the law of the United States, or under the law of a country where the content can be accessed;
  • is pornographic or sexually explicit, or depicts, describes or simulates sexual acts, nudity, sexual body parts, fetish content, or sexual role play;
  • is sexually suggestive, including suggestive avatar poses, animations, clothing, sounds or descriptions;
  • depicts realistic or gratuitous violence, gore, mutilation, torture, animal cruelty, or the death or injury of real people;
  • promotes, celebrates or provides instructions for terrorism, violent extremism, mass violence or the acquisition or manufacture of weapons or explosives;
  • promotes, encourages or provides instruction for suicide, self-harm or disordered eating;
  • depicts or promotes the use of drugs, alcohol, tobacco, vaping or other controlled substances;
  • promotes, simulates or facilitates gambling, including wagering virtual or real items on chance;
  • is hateful, meaning that it attacks, demeans, dehumanises, excludes or incites hatred against a person or group on the basis of a protected characteristic;
  • harasses, bullies, threatens, intimidates, humiliates or targets any person;
  • reveals or seeks to reveal private information about any person, including real name, address, school, workplace, telephone number, email address, financial information, images of a person in a private setting, or precise location;
  • impersonates another person, a brand, a public figure, a DynaBlocks employee or moderator, or a DynaBlocks system message;
  • is deliberately misleading in a way that causes harm, including scams, fake giveaways, phishing, and false claims about what an item or experience does;
  • infringes copyright, trade mark or any other right of another person; or
  • is spam, including repetitive posting, mass unsolicited messaging, and content created only to manipulate discovery, rankings or search.

5.2 Political, religious and social topics are not banned, but DynaBlocks is not a campaigning platform. Content on these topics must still meet the age-appropriate standard in Section 4 and must not become hateful, harassing or a vehicle for real world conflict.

6. Child Safety

6.1 This is the rule we enforce most strictly and with the least tolerance. There is no warning, no second chance, and no appeal on the facts where the conduct is established.

6.2 The following are absolutely prohibited:

  • child sexual abuse material of any kind, in any form, including drawn, rendered, animated, textual or artificially generated depictions;
  • sexualising a child, or an avatar or character presented as a child, in any way;
  • grooming, meaning any attempt to build a relationship with a child in order to exploit, abuse or sexually solicit them;
  • sexual or romantic communication with a child, or attempts to initiate it;
  • asking a child for personal information, photographs, video, or contact details;
  • attempting to move a conversation with a child to another platform, to a private server, to a messaging application, or to any off-Platform channel;
  • encouraging a child to keep a conversation, relationship or gift secret from their parents, guardians or other trusted adults;
  • offering a child DynaBucks, items, in-experience advantages, gifts or money in exchange for personal contact, images, or any other benefit;
  • praising, normalising, defending or trivialising the sexual abuse or exploitation of children; and
  • threatening or attempting to expose, blackmail or coerce a child, including by threatening to share images or information.

6.3 Adults using DynaBlocks should interact with children on the Platform only in the ordinary course of playing and creating, and in public. If you are an adult and a child contacts you privately, keep it appropriate, keep it on the Platform, and report anything that concerns you.

6.4 If you are a child and an adult, or anyone at all, says something that makes you uncomfortable, asks you for personal information or photographs, asks you to move to another app, offers you things in exchange for something, or asks you to keep a secret, do not reply. Use the report button and tell a parent, guardian, teacher or another adult you trust. You will never be in trouble with us for reporting.

6.5 We report suspected child sexual exploitation to the appropriate authorities and relevant child-safety organisations where required or appropriate. We cooperate with lawful investigations.

6.6 We may remove accounts held by registered sex offenders where we become aware of them, subject to applicable law.

7. Communication Rules

7.1 Chat, messaging, groups and voice are the parts of DynaBlocks where harm is most likely to reach a child directly, so the rules here are strict.

7.2 Language. Do not use vulgar, obscene, profane or crude language. Do not use slurs. Do not use sexual language. Do not use insults directed at a person or group. Our filters block a wide range of terms, and deliberately working around a filter, by misspelling, spacing, substituting characters, using another language, or using coded terms, is a breach in its own right.

7.3 Hate speech is prohibited without exception. That includes slurs and demeaning content targeting a person or group based on race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, disability, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, or any other protected characteristic, and it includes hateful symbols, jokes framed as humour, and "ironic" use.

7.4 Do not spam, flood, repeat messages, send mass unsolicited messages, or use chat to advertise off-Platform services.

7.5 Do not share personal information, yours or anyone else's. That includes full names, addresses, schools, phone numbers, email addresses, social media handles, video call links, photographs of yourself or others, and payment details.

7.6 Do not direct another user off the Platform, particularly a user who may be a child. Do not solicit or share invitations to private servers or external chat services.

7.7 Chat on DynaBlocks is not private. It may be filtered before it is delivered, stored, reviewed by automated systems, and read by moderators for safety purposes. Assume it will be read.

8. Voice Chat

8.1 Where voice chat is available to you, everything in Section 7 applies to what you say as well as to what you type.

8.2 Voice chat may be subject to age restrictions and to age assurance before it becomes available to you, and it may not be available on every account.

8.3 Voice audio may be recorded and retained for a limited period, and may be analyzed by automated systems, so that reports can be investigated. The Privacy Policy explains how that information is handled.

8.4 Do not play music, sound effects, background audio or media through voice chat that you do not have the rights to, that is not age-appropriate, or that is designed to disrupt.

8.5 Do not use voice chat to pressure another user to reveal their age, gender, location or appearance, and do not use it to make another user uncomfortable about their voice.

9. Harassment, Bullying and Threats

9.1 Do not target another user with repeated unwanted contact, insults, mockery, exclusion campaigns, or pile-ons.

9.2 Do not organise or participate in brigading, mass reporting, coordinated raids on an experience or group, or campaigns to drive a user off the Platform.

9.3 Do not threaten violence against any person, and do not make statements about harming a person, a place, a school or an event, whether or not you intend to act on them. We treat credible threats as a safety matter and may report them.

9.4 Do not publish or threaten to publish private information about another person, and do not make false reports to emergency services about another person.

9.5 Do not sexually harass another user, including through unwanted sexual comments, requests, imagery or persistent romantic advances.

9.6 Trash talk within a competitive game is not automatically harassment. Personal attacks, slurs, and anything aimed at a person rather than at the game are.

10. Self-Harm, Suicide and Eating Disorders

10.1 Do not encourage, promote, instruct in, glorify or normalise suicide, self-harm or disordered eating, and do not challenge, dare or pressure another user in relation to any of them.

10.2 Do not create experiences, items or content that depict, simulate or make a game of these subjects.

10.3 If you are struggling, you are not in trouble and you are not doing anything wrong by talking to someone. Please tell a parent, guardian or another trusted adult, or contact a support service in your country. If you are worried about another user, report the content so that our safety team can see it, and if you believe someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services.

10.4 We may remove content of this kind, restrict the account that posted it, and surface support information, and we do this to protect people rather than to punish them.

11. Platform Integrity: Exploits, Cheating and Automation

11.1 Do not modify the behavior of the DynaBlocks player application, the client software, the game engine or our servers. That includes injectors, memory editors, packet manipulation, unauthorized third party clients, modified binaries, and any tool that changes how the Platform is intended to work.

11.2 Do not use, create, sell, distribute or advertise cheats, exploits, hacks, aimbots, wall hacks, speed hacks, teleporters, duplication glitches or DynaBucks generators.

11.3 Do not exploit a bug or fault for advantage. If you find one, report it to DynaBlocks rather than using it or publicizing it.

11.4 Do not automate account activity. That includes automated account creation, automated friending, following, joining, messaging, posting, purchasing, trading, play time inflation, and any bot, script or macro that simulates a person using the Platform.

11.5 Do not manipulate discovery, rankings, visit counts, ratings, reviews, favorites or earnings by artificial means, including alternate accounts, paid engagement or coordinated groups.

11.6 Do not attack the Platform or its users, including through denial of service attacks, attempts to gain unauthorized access to accounts or systems, phishing, credential stuffing, malware, or scanning and probing our infrastructure.

11.7 Do not evade an enforcement action, including by creating a new account after a ban, using another person's account, or circumventing a device or network level restriction.

12. Commercial Conduct, Scams and Trading

12.1 Describe what you sell accurately. Do not misrepresent what an item or experience contains, does or unlocks, and do not change what a paid item provides after the sale in a way that materially reduces its value.

12.2 Do not run scams. That includes fake giveaways, fake middleman or "trust trade" arrangements, false promises of DynaBucks or items, phishing links, and impersonating DynaBlocks staff, moderators or support.

12.3 Do not buy, sell, trade, gift, auction or advertise accounts, DynaBucks or virtual items for real world money or other consideration outside the Platform. Off-Platform trading is where users can lose their items, and we may not be able to help recover them.

12.4 Do not use DynaBlocks trading features to launder value, settle off-Platform debts, transfer value between accounts to avoid enforcement, or move funds obtained through fraud.

12.5 Do not target children with high pressure sales tactics, manipulative purchase prompts, false urgency, or designs intended to cause accidental spending.

12.6 Do not advertise or promote products or services that are unlawful or unsuitable for a general audience that includes children, including gambling, alcohol, tobacco, vaping, controlled substances and weapons.

12.7 If you have a commercial relationship behind content you promote on the Platform, disclose it clearly.

13. Intellectual Property and Impersonation

13.1 Upload only what you have the right to upload. That means work you made yourself, work you have permission to use, or work that is genuinely free for you to use on the terms attached to it.

13.2 Do not upload copyrighted characters, logos, music, sound effects, models, textures or code without permission, and do not rebuild a protected work asset by asset and present it as your own.

13.3 Do not use another person's or company's trade mark, name or branding in a way that suggests an association, sponsorship or endorsement that does not exist.

13.4 Do not impersonate another user, a real person, a brand, a DynaBlocks employee or moderator, or a DynaBlocks system message, and do not create an account, group or experience designed to be mistaken for one of those.

13.5 Copyright owners can submit a takedown notice as described in the DynaBlocks Copyright Notice, and users whose content is removed in error can submit a counter-notification. Users who repeatedly infringe may lose their accounts.

14. Reporting and What Happens Next

14.1 Use the report tools in the Platform to report a user, an experience, an item, a message, a group or a piece of content. Copyright complaints should be submitted through the designated DynaBlocks copyright reporting process.

14.2 Tell us what you saw, where you saw it and when. Screenshots, usernames, experience names and timestamps help us act quickly and accurately.

14.3 Reports are treated confidentially where possible. We do not normally tell the reported user who reported them.

14.4 Reports of child safety concerns, credible threats of violence and content involving self-harm are prioritised over all other reports.

14.5 Do not abuse the reporting system. Mass reporting, coordinated false reporting and reporting a user out of spite are themselves breaches of these Guidelines.

14.6 We will act on a report where it is justified. We may not be able to tell you what action we took about another person's account, but you will receive an acknowledgement of the report where the Platform provides one.

15. Enforcement, Consequences and Appeals

15.1 Detection. We use a combination of automated systems, including chat filters and machine learning classifiers, and human moderators. Automated systems can block or remove content before a person sees it. Neither approach is perfect.

15.2 What we can do. Depending on what happened, we may:

  • issue a warning;
  • remove, hide or unpublish content;
  • restrict a feature such as chat, voice, trading, publishing or purchasing;
  • remove DynaBucks or items obtained in breach of the rules;
  • withhold or forfeit creator earnings connected to the breach;
  • suspend the account for a period;
  • permanently terminate the account; and
  • apply a device or network level restriction to prevent the ban being evaded.

15.3 How we decide. We look at what the conduct was, how serious the harm was or could have been, whether it was deliberate, whether it was repeated, whether our filters were deliberately evaded, and the age of the people involved. Most first breaches of the milder rules result in content removal and a warning. Repeated breaches escalate.

15.4 No tolerance categories. Child sexual exploitation, grooming, credible threats of violence, and the distribution of malware or of another user's private information may result in immediate permanent termination without a prior warning, and may be reported to the authorities where appropriate.

15.5 What you are told. Where we take action against your content or your account, we will tell you what we did and, so far as we are able, why, unless telling you would be unlawful, would prejudice an investigation, or would put someone at risk.

15.6 Appeals. You can appeal an enforcement decision through the DynaBlocks support or appeals process within 90 days. An appeal is reviewed by someone who was not responsible for the original decision, where that decision was automated or where the appeal raises a question of judgment. If we get it wrong, we may restore your content, lift the restriction and restore any items or DynaBucks removed as a result.

15.7 Users in the European Union may have additional rights in relation to moderation decisions, including rights that may apply under applicable digital services laws.

15.8 Changes. We will update these Guidelines as the Platform grows and as new risks appear. The current version will be available on the DynaBlocks website, and material changes may be notified through the Platform.
